A broadsheet front page for Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn and typeset from scratch — no photographs, no stock art — summing up the day's world news in a single 1024×1024 image. The masthead names the day in large type; below it, six stories, each with a small hand-drawn picture and a caption: the International Court of Justice ruling Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; Xi Jinping meeting Donald Trump in Shenzhen and warning on Taiwan; Anthropic's record Nasdaq debut as ANT; Indonesia's parliament passing an asset forfeiture law; a migrant boat capsizing off Senegal with dozens missing; and a wave of hacking and hoax attacks hitting Italy. A markets strip along the foot closes the page. Every story is stated as the day's reporting had it, cross-checked against Reuters, AP, CNN, BBC, Al Jazeera and Dow Jones Newswires / Morningstar.
